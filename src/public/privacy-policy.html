Privacy Policy

BlackNote — Chrome Extension · Last updated: April 23, 2026

1. Overview

BlackNote is a side-panel note-taking Chrome extension with an integrated AI writing assistant and optional cloud sync. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about the data we handle.

2. Data We Collect

BlackNote collects and processes the following data:

  • Notes & Clipped Content: The text, images, and media you create within the editor, as well as articles you explicitly capture using the Web Clipper. Notes are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) by default. If you sign in, notes and images are also synced to our secure cloud database (AWS DynamoDB) via our backend API for cross-device access.
  • Account information: If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your email address, display name, and profile picture from Google. This is used solely for authentication and displaying your profile within the extension.
  • AI prompts: When you use the AI writing assistant, the text you select or type is sent to our backend server for processing. We do not store or log the content of your AI prompts beyond what is needed to generate a response.
  • Usage metadata: For users with AI credits, we log the number of credits consumed and the AI model used. This is strictly for billing and service monitoring purposes. No note content is included in these logs.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect browsing history. The Web Clipper only reads the specific webpage you are actively viewing, and only after you explicitly click the "Clip Page" button.
  • We do not track, profile, or sell your personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, affiliate links, or tracking scripts.
  • We do not read or access any data from other browser tabs or extensions.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Note storage & Web Clipping: To persist your notes and captured web articles locally and synchronize them across devices via cloud storage.
  • Authentication: To identify your account and associate your notes with your profile.
  • AI processing: To send your selected text to the AI model and return the generated response.
  • Credit management: To track AI usage credits for premium users.

5. Third-Party Services

BlackNote uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Identity Service — For secure user authentication (Google OAuth via Chrome Identity API).
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS DynamoDB) — For secure cloud database storage. AWS Privacy Policy
  • Google Cloud (Vertex AI / Gemini) — For AI text generation on our backend server. Google Cloud Privacy Notice
  • Lemon Squeezy — For payment processing of AI credit purchases. We do not store your payment information; it is handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy. Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy

6. Data Storage & Security

  • Local notes are stored in your browser's IndexedDB and never leave your device unless you sign in for cloud sync.
  • Cloud data is stored securely in AWS DynamoDB with strict access controls — only your authenticated account can access your own notes.
  • All network communication uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Our backend is protected with authentication middleware, rate limiting, and security headers (Helmet).

7. Your Rights

  • Delete your data: You can delete individual notes within the extension. To delete all cloud data, sign out and contact us.
  • Export your data: Your notes are stored in standard JSON format and can be accessed via your browser's developer tools.
  • Revoke access: You can sign out at any time. You can also revoke Google access from your Google Account settings.

8. Permissions Explained

  • sidePanel: Required to display the note-taking interface as a browser side panel.
  • identity: Required for Google Sign-in to enable cloud sync functionality.
  • activeTab: Required for the Web Clipper. This permission allows the extension to interact with the webpage you are currently viewing, strictly on-demand when you choose to clip the page.
  • Host Permissions (<all_urls>): Necessary to allow the Web Clipper to extract articles from any website you choose to clip across the internet.
